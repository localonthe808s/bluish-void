## 7. Fix on this branch

`make_lock()` stamped `'at'` with a literal `' ET'` while `now` is the market's local clock. The file's `updated`
field had the same bug, and it was already fixed at line ~4977. So the LAS and AUS locks claimed a time 3 h or 1 h
earlier than they were made.
- Before: AUS 10-01 lock `"at": "2026-10-01T12:01 ET"`, `"as_of": "12:00 CT"`. Its trail row is `lt 12:01` = 17:01Z =
  13:01 ET.
- After: `"at": "2026-10-01T12:01 CT"` (from `cfg['tzlabel']`, the same as `as_of`).

The change is one line (`_kalshi/kalshi_daily.py:4382`). The sheet's `stamp12()` keeps any suffix, and nothing
parses the suffix. py_compile passes. Locks already written are not rewritten.
