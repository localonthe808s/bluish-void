## 2. The trail (2026-10-02)

- **AUS high, wrong-way confidence in the evening.** From 17:01 to 18:55 CT, 24 rows had sd 0.30 with pred 79.98-80.38
  sitting above the floor (`hmax` 78.9-79.0). The pick was 80-81 at p 0.946-0.998, for example 17:51: p 0.9983, CLI
  79 at 4:32 PM, own5 78.8, market on 78-79. The cause, read from `kalshi_daily.py` ~4040: when `peak_done.p >= 0.85`
  and the 5-minute feed is in hand and the day is *not* binding, the spread becomes `max(0.30, gap5_sd + 0.15)`. But
  `pred` stays at `fadj`, the forecast, which is still above the observed maximum. So the spread is cut to "only the
  5-minute gap is left" while the centre still assumes more heating. `peak_stats.json` for aus_high has
  `p_done`=1.0 from 17 h and `gap5_sd` 0.0 (15 days), so the collapse hits the floor value of 0.30. The pick went
  78-79 at 18:01, back to 80-81 at 18:45 (0.946), and back to 78-79 at 19:01. It was decided at 19:31.
  - Replaying the same shape on the trail, 09-24..10-02 (27 city-days): rows with peak_p >= 0.85, not decided, and
    pred more than 0.3 above `hmax` appear on AUS 09-26 (12 rows, right), LAS 09-29 (10 rows, 4 at p>0.6 on 82-83
    against a settled 80), and AUS 10-02 (24 rows). Centring on the floor would have fixed LAS 09-29 and AUS 10-02,
    but it would have put AUS 09-26 at 17:01 on 92-93 against a settled 94. So this is a modelling choice and not a
    one-line bug, and it is left for the owner.

## 7. Fix on this branch

`trail_row()` reads `doc['today']['own5_row']` to log `own5_last` and `own5_at`. But `_run_market()` never writes
that key, so both fields have been null in every trail row: 0 of about 5,500 rows across 27 city-days, against 3,700
rows that have `own5`. That is the reading the cooling and day-decided tests use, which is why the LAS 17:05 reversal
above cannot be explained from the record.

One line in `_kalshi/kalshi_daily.py` (the `today` doc, beside `own5_max`): `'own5_row': own5_row(cfg, today) if
_own5 is not None else None`. `own5_row()` is served from the summary that `own5_max()` already fetched, so it makes
no new request. Nothing else reads the key.

- Before, `trail_row(kalshi_las.json, 21:57)`: `{'own5': 95.0, 'own5_last': None, 'own5_at': None}`
- After, with the worker's row (`{'max7': 95, 'maxAt': '15:20', 'last': 84.2, 'at': '21:40', ...}`):
  `{'own5': 95.0, 'own5_last': 84.2, 'own5_at': '21:40'}`

The change does not touch the forecast, the floor or any learned value. py_compile passes.
