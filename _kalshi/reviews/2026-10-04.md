- **The 5-minute floor froze in both 5-minute cities.** In LAS, `obs.own5` sat at 75.2 from 07:21 to 15:36 PT. In AUS it
  sat at 75.92 from 08:21 to 17:36 CT. On 10-02 the same field moved 15 times in LAS and 6 times in AUS. New York's

  - Cause not established from here. Two candidates, neither proven:
    - The KV write budget. 10-03 was the first day the dark-flow watcher wrote every minute (ff48237, 13:02Z). Against this,
      recovery came at 22:35Z, not at the 00:00Z reset.

      01:38Z on 10-03 (70fcd74), with a 12 s abort, while the weather-service fetch has no timeout.
  - The worker logs for `[obs-log]` lines between 14:36Z and 22:35Z on 10-03 will tell which. If it was the KV budget,
    the dark watcher's empty first day (`kalshi_dark.json` days {}, n 0) may be the same outage.
