## 7. Fix

None. The Austin sd collapse has a clear mechanism, but any fix changes how a learned value (`gap5_sd`) and the
binding-floor rule set the spread. That is a guardrail change for the owner, not a surgical bug fix. Two options
for the owner:
(a) do not collapse the spread on a binding 5-minute floor while `peak_p` is below some bar;
(b) treat a `gap5_sd` of 0.0 measured on fewer than 20 days as unmeasured.
