## 2. The trail (2026-10-20 and 2026-10-21, ny_high)

- **The CDN is stale without a cache-buster.** A plain GET of `cdn.bluishvoid.com/kalshi/trail/ny_high_2026-10-21.jsonl`
  returned 74 rows ending 06:05 local, with `age: 86311`, `cf-cache-status: HIT`, `last-modified` 10-21 10:06Z, even though
  `cache-control: max-age=120`. With `?v=<random>` the same URL returned 290 rows ending 23:55 (MISS). On 10-20 it was 73 rows
  plain against 290 fresh. `kalshi/live/kalshi_ny.json`, `kalshi_low_ny.json` and `kalshi_report.json` plain were all the
  10-21 06:05 ET bake (age 86297) at 10:05Z on 10-22. So something at the Cloudflare edge (likely an Edge TTL / cache rule)
  overrides the origin's max-age. Who is affected:
  - The sheet: not affected. Every `_kxFetch` call in index.html passes `?v=...`.
  - `trail_study.py`: not affected. It sends `?v=<day>`, and 10-20 came in with 290 bakes.
  - `insider_study.py:202` and `kalshi_daily.py:3586` (`trail_rows`, used by `execution_score`) fetch with no query.
    `execution_score` is inert because `trades.csv` has no fills. insider_study's TWC rows for recent days may be
    truncated.
  - This README's step 2 URL also has no query, so a reviewer following it reads the truncated copy.
  The fix is the CDN rule, outside this repo. I made no code change.
- On the full (busted) trails: 10-20 held p 0.65-0.71 on 65-66 from 00:00 to at least 06:00, and the day settled 67-68.
  That is wrong-way confidence, but it fits the low-bias run above, not a separate bug. 10-21 had no p > 0.6 on a loser.
