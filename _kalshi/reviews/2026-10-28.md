## 7. The fix on this branch

`_kalshi/kalshi_daily.py`, afternoon blend: the climb table's outcomes are dropped where the cut already removed them
(`t > cut_far - 1.0`, the same rule `distribution()` applies through `cut = floor - 0.5`). If nothing survives, the
blend leaves the ladder as it was. No learned value, weight or guardrail is touched.
