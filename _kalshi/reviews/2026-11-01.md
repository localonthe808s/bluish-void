## 2. The trail (2026-10-31)

**Finding (high): the CDN serves a stale, truncated trail for about 24 h.** `cdn.bluishvoid.com/kalshi/trail/ny_high_2026-10-31.jsonl`
returns `cf-cache-status: HIT`, `age: 86579`, `last-modified: Sat, 31 Oct 2026 10:01:54 GMT`, 73 rows ending 06:00 ET,
even though the object sets `cache-control: public, max-age=120`. The same URL with a query string returns 290 rows
ending 23:55 ET. The R2 object itself is whole: the 14:32Z run logged "appended 1 row -> ny_high_2026-10-31.jsonl
(127 total)". Truncated days found: 10-26, 10-29, 10-30 and 10-31 end at 06:00-06:05 ET; 10-27 and 10-28 end at 07:00.
10-25 was whole (281 rows).

Who reads it unbusted:

## 3. What learned overnight

The nightly refit 80486d840 (10-31 15:32Z) set ny_high `sd_mult` from 1.25 to 1.0. Its `why` reads: "0.75 refused:
1.247 ... against 1.144 for 1.0, which they were made with; step to 1.25 waits: the last live step was 2026-10-30".
So the why names a wait, yet the value went back down. On the court's own scores, 1.25 (1.102) and 1.5 (1.074) both
beat 1.0 (1.144).

Cause, in `tune.py` `live_spread_court`:
1. `made_with` is the most common sd_mult among the last 14 lock stamps. That is still 1.0, because only one lock was
   made at 1.25.
2. A refused search choice falls back to `made_with`, not to the court's own last ruling.
3. The cooldown then blocks the step back up.

The same thing happened on 10-23: the step to 1.25 took effect for one lock (10-23 `params.sd_mult` 1.25), and the
10-24 lock was back at 1.0. As written, a live step cannot outlast one night until 8 of 14 locks carry it. Not fixed:
it is a tuner rule, and tonight's single fix is spent on the trail. The owner should decide.

The other three commits (public-forecast pull, blend shadows, tail margin) are new code by the owner. Each states it
starts dry or off. Studies moved little: trail_study 53 -> 54 days, stable_to_lock 7/21.
