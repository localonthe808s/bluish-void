**Finding (medium): `over` fired at 08:55 with p 0.86 on the losing bracket, and held for an hour.**

- At 08:55 the 08:51 hourly read 59.0. The floor became 59.0 + 0.775 = 59.775, which was at or above the morning point forecast. That set `binding_now` (`kalshi_daily.py:4641`), which does three things:
  - It collapsed sd from 0.808 to 0.651.
  - It raised p on 59-60 from 0.784 to 0.864.
  - It set `day_over` (`kalshi_daily.py:5757`; trail `over`), which by its own comment means "the day's warming is finished".
- `over` stayed true on 59-60 from 08:55 to 09:50 (12 rows).
- The 09:51 hourly read 60.08, and the CLI high was 61 at 10:14.
- This is the second day running that the floor-against-forecast bind fired while the peak was still ahead (11-11: noon lock bound at p 0.87 and missed).
- **What the two days share:** the model blend has been too cold for 5 days, so a morning reading reaches the cold forecast early and trips the bind.
  - On 11-12 the bound noon lock (60.855) happened to land in the right bracket.
  - On 11-11 it did not.
- This is a design rule, not a coding error, so I changed nothing. The evidence is for the owner. A bind that fires before the forecast peak hour, while the bias runs cold, is what is worth a look.

- The pick never changed. The widening is the documented rule ("a forecast that never arrived must NOT collapse the spread", `kalshi_daily.py:5022`).
