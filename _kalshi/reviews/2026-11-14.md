## 2. The trail (2026-11-13)

**CDN:** the cache-busted and unbusted reads were byte-identical, both cf-cache MISS. ny_high has 281 rows (00:06-23:46) and ny_low has 284 (00:03-23:56). 0 rows had the wrong date. One 30-minute gap at 01:26 shows in both trails. six and cli never moved backwards.

### Finding (medium, fixed on this branch): the afternoon blend put mass under the exact floor

- **What the trail shows.** At 15:40 `dsm` arrived at 52.0 (later the CLI too), so `hmax` became 52.0, pred 52.0 and sd 0.399. Yet the ladder's `ours` read **0.672 on "49 or below"**, 0.073 on 50-51 and 0.255 on 52-53. It stayed like that until 17:55, rising to 0.686 at 16:25.
- **The plain ladder was right.** It cuts at `cut_far` = 52, so it had 52-53 at 1.0. At 18:00 `decided_by_exact` collapsed the day to 52-53 at 1.0.
- **Cause.** The afternoon blend (`kalshi_daily.py`, after `ladder_probs`) mixes in the climb table at `emp_w`, which is 0.75 on a binding 3-5 PM per `price_study.json` `afternoon_blend.ny_high.bind`. The climb table is centred on `round(hourly run)` = 48 and was never cut. So 75% of the ladder sat on 48-50, which every exact source said was impossible.
- **Fix.** Drop climb-table outcomes below the ladder's own cut (`t >= cut_far - 0.5`, the same cut `distribution()` applies), 4 lines.
  - It is a no-op whenever the cut is the hourly run itself, because `round(run) >= run - 0.5`.
  - It acts only when an exact figure (or the 5-minute max) sits more than half a degree above the rounded run.
  - If every climb outcome is cut, the blend falls back to the plain ladder.
  - `kalshi_lows.py` has no climb blend, so it needs no change.
- **Before/after replay.** This uses the committed `afternoon.json` (SON rows) and emp_w 0.75. The "before" replay reproduces the trail to 4 decimals. Brackets are 49 or below, 50-51, 52-53 and 54-55.

  | row | trail (before) | after fix |

  | 15:40 | 0.672 / 0.0726 / 0.2554 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 1.0 / 0 |
  | 16:25 | 0.6863 / 0.0602 / 0.2382 / 0.0154 | 0 / 0 / 0.9845 / 0.0154 |

- **Caveat, plainly.** If the final CLI's 47 stands at settlement, the old blend's wrong-way number would have landed on the winner by accident. The fix is still right on the code's own terms: everything else on the sheet, including the 18:00 decision, treats the DSM figure as an exact lower bound. If that figure is not trustworthy, the remedy belongs in the floor, not in a blend that quietly contradicts it.

### Finding (medium, no fix): the boundary straddle never runs in standard time

- **The day's shape.** 11-13 was a front night: 51 at 11:51 PM, 48 at 12:51 AM, and the preliminary high of 52 at 12:11 AM.
- **What the sheet did.** It bound at 00:56 on 48.795 (the hourly 48.02 plus 0.775) and called the day over: p 0.857 on "49 or below", sd 0.651. It held that through the noon lock, while the market sat on 52-53 at 0.6-0.9 all day.
- **Cause.** `boundary_straddle` (`kalshi_daily.py:895`) starts with `if not h0 ... return None`. After the 11-01 switch to EST, h0 (the climate day's first hour) is 0, so the rule never runs. Its measured evidence is also daylight-time only (40 front nights, 1 AM boundary).
- **Why no fix.** Extending it to the midnight boundary needs the previous date's 23:51 reading and its own measurement. That is a design change for the owner, not a surgical fix.
