## 7. The fix (carried again from claude/kalshi-review-2026-11-17)

In `_kalshi/kalshi_daily.py`, the guard goes right after the existing `no forecast and no observations yet` exit in
`_run_market`. When no model has any hour of today, the bake prints a line and returns 0. The last good bake then
stays on the sheet, and the noon lock is made by the first run at or after noon that has a forecast. The patch is
identical to the one on the 11-17 branch, and it applies cleanly to current main.

**Before/after.** I replayed current main's `forecast_runs` offline for ny_high with six models. The archive was
stubbed to end yesterday, and the live call either answers or raises the logged handshake timeout:

| live call | models with an hour today | guard |
|---|---|---|
| answers | 6 of 6 | does not fire, so the bake proceeds as before |
| times out | 0 of 6 | fires, so the run does not write and the last bake stands |

On the trail, the guard would have suppressed exactly the `models: null` rows: 52 on 11-17 and 11 so far on 11-18.
That includes the current 05:00 row (58-59 at 0.63). With it, the sheet would show the 04:55 bake instead
(62-63 at 0.65).
