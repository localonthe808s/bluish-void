(`kalshi_daily.py:577`): it needs `peak_done.p >= 0.95`. `_kalshi/peak_stats.json` ny_high (n_days 37) gives

the figure, and every hourly report after the DSM's as-of was more than a degree under it. The figure on the sheet
was derived (the hourly max plus the mean peak gap), not a reading, and two exact readings contradicted it.
The noon lock was not affected. The sheet was. The 0.95 gate and the table are learned values or guardrails, so they
are left as they are. Whether the gate should depend on the exact figure being in hand is the owner's call.
