53.3, ICON 51.3, ECMWF 54.7, GEM 55.7. So `snapshot` in `_kalshi/kalshi_lows.py` (`bind = fl >= pf`) treated the
hour as binding. On a binding hour from LOCK_HOUR on, the bracket odds come from the live gap table
(`live_gap_table`, built from the 8 AM binding locks): settled = round(reading) + g. With the reading at 52, that put
0.93 on "51 or above". The comment there says that table's two misses were "day's-end drops", and this was one. The
reading at that hour was not a floor: the air had fallen from 55 to 52 in two hours. 11-23 is not settled yet, but
the hourly minimum in hand (50.0) already rules out "51 or above". The pick also flipped 10 times after noon. The 8 AM
lock (49-50) is not affected. The gap table, the binding rule and LOCK_HOUR are learned values or guardrails, so they
are not changed. Whether a binding hour late in the evening needs its own table is the owner's call.
