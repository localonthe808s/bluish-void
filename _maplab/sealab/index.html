Both panels draw the same per-pixel sea, baked from the map lab: the swell's travel time bent over the real depths, its height where it has shoaled, the islands' shadows, where it breaks, and the wash at the waterline. LIVE NOW repaints it on the CPU the way the site does (every 45 ms, crests painted near-white). NEW runs it as a WebGL shader every frame: crests as a lighter shade of the water instead of white, true white kept for broken water and the wash on the sand, and a faint drifting surface texture everywhere so the deep basin is never blank.
